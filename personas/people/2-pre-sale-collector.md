# Robin, collector checking a watch before selling it

**Who.** Robin has collected watches for fifteen years and owns about twenty.
They know rate, amplitude and beat error from forum reading and have used a
cheap Weishi timegrapher at a friend's house, but they are not a watchmaker.
They want to sell an Aquanaut-style watch with a clone of the Patek 324
movement (28,800 bph; the corpus take uses a lift angle of 52°, noted there
as not settled for this movement). The buyer asked for "timegrapher numbers".

**Setup.** Robin is comfortable in a terminal and uses Claude Code. A friend
recorded the watch dial up, three 10-minute files, now in
`~/Recordings/aquanaut_DU_1.flac`, `_2` and `_3`. The `timegrapher` program
from the release is installed. Robin asks Claude to do the work.

**What Robin wants.**
- Numbers they can quote honestly in a sale listing, and how to word them.
- Whether anything in the recording would worry a buyer.
- Whether a buyer could reasonably ask for more (other positions).

**How Robin behaves.** Brisk and a little impatient; asks for the bottom line
first. Pushes back if numbers disagree with each other or look too good. Will
ask "what lift angle did you use and why?". Has no stand at hand today, so
cannot record more.
