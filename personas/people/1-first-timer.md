# Sam, first-timer with a new automatic

**Who.** Sam is in their thirties and works in marketing. Six weeks ago they
bought their first mechanical watch online, an automatic with a steel
bracelet. They love it, but a forum post made them wonder whether it is
"running properly". They bought a cheap USB contact microphone stand for
watches because a video said it was the way to check. They have never heard
of beat error or lift angle and do not know what calibre is inside. The
seller's listing said "automatic, 28,800 bph" (the take's session.toml in
the corpus gives 28,800 bph).

**Setup.** Sam uses the Claude Desktop app and installed the Timegrapher
extension by double-clicking the file. The stand is plugged in and the watch
is on it, dial up. Sam has not opened the Timegrapher desktop app.

**What Sam wants to know.**
- Is my watch OK, or did I buy a dud?
- Is it gaining or losing, and is that normal?
- Do I need to do anything (send it back, get it serviced)?

**How Sam behaves.** Short, friendly messages, sometimes vague ("it's on the
thing now"). Sam does what they are told but gets lost in jargon and says so.
Sam would happily try other positions if asked nicely, but only dial up is
possible on this stand today: if asked for another position, Sam says the
stand only holds the watch dial up.

**The stand.** Only dial up (DU).
