This is a test build for a small group of testers. It is not a public release.

Download the file for your computer below. The steps for opening it the first time are in [docs/install.md](https://github.com/peterhudson/timegrapher/blob/main/docs/install.md). The builds are unsigned, so macOS and Windows will warn you once.

## What's new since 0.2.0

### Facts, not verdicts
- The app, the command line tool and the assistant's chat views now show what was measured, with its ± and its limits, and leave the judgement to you. There are no grades, no tolerance colours, no named faults and no advice.
- The confidence dots and the High, Medium and Low grades from 0.2.0 are gone. Each reading shows its ± and says plainly what limits it, for example clipping, missing beats or a very short stretch.
- The Positions Test table no longer marks readings against Men's, Ladies' or COSC tolerances, and the findings with advice are gone.

### Positions Test
- The Positions Test has a pane of its own, with a plain table. Each button names the position it is for.
- Positions carry the Swiss laboratory names beside the crown positions, for example Crown Up (CU, 3H).
- **Save as CSV…** and **Copy** put the table, with the watch, calibre, beat rate, lift angle and timings, into a spreadsheet or on the clipboard.
- **Full wind against 24 hours.** After a test run just after winding, **Save Results for 24 Hours…** keeps its results. A test run 24 hours later loads them and adds a Full Wind Against 24 Hours table with each position's rate and amplitude then and now, and the change.
- Saved takes are named after the watch and calibre.

### Calibres
- The calibre list now holds the full lift-angle table, and you can search it by any name a calibre goes by.
- The Calibre menu keeps to your recent picks, a short list by maker and your own calibres. **Manage Calibres…** opens a window with every calibre, where you tick the ones you want on the short list and add calibres of your own.
- Peacock, Jinghe, Seagull and Hangzhou calibres are added from the makers' own pages.
- A form on GitHub lets you report a wrong or missing calibre.

### Steadiness, Periods and FFT
- The Steadiness views are panes of their own, and there is a new FFT view.
- Periods and FFT mark the wheels' periods in bold on a pale grid, with an optional log scale. The FFT reads in s/d.

### The window and the charts
- Each pane has its own toolbar and icon, there is a menu bar, and the app has its own icon.
- A Watch pane holds the sound input, the take and its controls, and a Beat Rate card joins the reading cards.
- Charts rescale to their data and label their scales. Each pane has its own Average Over.
- The paper strip shows the time at its cursor line.
- **Save As…** copies a take to a new folder, and a recording can be replayed from half speed to 16 times speed.

### True Clock
- While it listens, the app times the microphone's clock against the computer's. After about 20 minutes, once that timing is steady, the Rate card offers to **Keep** the microphone's clock error. From then on a line under the rate gives it corrected onto true time.

### Fixes
- Long takes no longer crash when a stored clock correction is used.
- Tick and Tock keep their sides across a gap in the sound.
- Recordings open with fresh settings, and sound is never lost without a warning.
- An empty stand says no watch is heard again.
- On Linux under KDE Wayland, the taskbar shows the Timegrapher icon once the app's menu entry is in place (Help > Add to Applications Menu).
- `timegrapher update` says the program is up to date while no release has been published, instead of reporting an error.

### For AI assistants
- The chat views and the assistant's guide state the program's facts and leave the judgement to the watchmaker's sources, without grades, tolerances or advice of their own.
- The chat's FFT and Periods views match the app's, with hover hints on the Log Scale and Timing switches.
